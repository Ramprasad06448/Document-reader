HOSPITAL DISCHARGE SUMMARY & CLINICAL ASSESSMENT

PATIENT HISTORY & DIAGNOSIS:
The patient was admitted for observation regarding recurrent episodic cephalalgia and moderate essential hypertension. Comprehensive radiographic imaging demonstrated a benign intracranial mass with no signs of malignant progression or adjacent edema.

ETIOLOGY & PROGNOSIS:
Laboratory markers revealed an idiopathic elevation in hepatic transaminases. The attending physician discussed the long-term prognosis, noting that the condition is currently asymptomatic and expected to stabilize with pharmacological maintenance. 

TREATMENT PLAN & CONTRAINDICATION:
Patient is discharged on an ACE inhibitor regimen. High-dose NSAID administration is subject to a strict contraindication due to prior gastric ulceration. Regular ambulatory monitoring is advised prior to resuming high-intensity athletic activities.
