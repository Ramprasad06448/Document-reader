COMMERCIAL LEASE & INDEMNIFICATION AGREEMENT

SECTION 1: PARTIES & RECITALS
Heretofore, Lessor and Lessee have engaged in commercial tenancy negotiations. In the event that either party fails to satisfy the conditions precedent herein, this agreement shall be deemed null and void.

SECTION 2: INDEMNIFICATION & LIABILITY
The Lessee covenants and agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Lessor, its agents, and assigns, from and against any and all claims, liabilities, losses, damages, and costs (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or subsequent to the Lessee's occupancy or use of the demised premises, notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained herein.

SECTION 3: DEFAULT & LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
In accordance with local regulations, prior to initiating formal termination, Lessor shall provide written notice. Subsequent to thirty (30) days of non-cure, Lessor may terminate this agreement in its sole discretion. The parties agree that the sum of $5,000 shall constitute liquidated damages and not a penalty, representing a genuine pre-estimate of damages.

SECTION 4: FORCE MAJEURE & ARBITRATION
Neither party shall be held liable for failure to perform its obligations for the reason that an act of God, war, pandemic, or force majeure event has occurred. All disputes arising out of or related to this agreement shall be resolved through final, binding arbitration under commercial arbitration rules. If any provision of this agreement is held invalid, the severability clause ensures all remaining sections remain in full force.
